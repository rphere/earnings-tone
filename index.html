We scored how upbeat executives sounded when answering analysts’ questions, compared it with how that company usually sounds, and checked whether the stock closed higher or lower on the next trading day.

About the data

Click to expand
  • Transcripts and per-answer sentiment scores (−1 to +1) come from Alpha Vantage. A call’s tone is the average score of the executives’ answers in the Q&A.
  • “Usual” is the company’s median score across all the calls shown, including later ones, so this is a first look rather than a tradable signal.
  • Quarters are the calendar quarter each call reported on. The fiscal quarter is shown next to each company in the table.
  • Next-day move is the stock’s return on the first trading day after the call. Quotes and summaries were picked by Claude Haiku.
